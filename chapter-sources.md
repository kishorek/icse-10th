## 1. Official syllabus

**CISCE — Regulations and Syllabuses ICSE 2027**

- Publisher: Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE)
- URL: https://cisce.org/regulations-and-syllabuses
- Relevant scope: Science Paper 2 (Chemistry), Unit 9 Organic Chemistry.
- Access note: the CISCE page returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval. The scope was therefore cross-checked against current 2026-27 syllabus summaries and the published 2025-26 wording.

The chapter scopes used in this project are:

- **Chapter 1 — Unit 9(i) introduction.** Introduction to organic compounds; unique nature of the carbon atom — tetravalency and catenation; formation of single, double and triple bonds; straight-chain, branched-chain and cyclic compounds (benzene structure only).
- **Chapter 12A — Unit 9(ii)–(iv).** Organic compounds and their sources; organic versus inorganic compounds; carbon's bonding; hydrocarbons; structural formulae; alkyl groups; functional groups; homologous series; IUPAC nomenclature; structural isomerism.
- **Chapter 12B — Unit 9(v), alkanes.** The alkane family and general formula; sources; chain isomerism; occurrence, structure, laboratory preparation and physical properties of methane and ethane; complete and incomplete combustion; substitution with chlorine; uses.
- **Chapter 12C — Unit 9(v), alkenes.** Alkenes as unsaturated hydrocarbons with one double bond; ethene as the example; preparation by dehydrohalogenation and dehydration; main properties, particularly addition with H₂, Cl₂, Br₂ and I₂; uses of ethene.
- **Chapter 12D — Unit 9(v), alkynes.** Alkynes as unsaturated hydrocarbons with one triple bond; ethyne as the example; preparation from calcium carbide and 1,2-dibromoethane; main properties, particularly addition with H₂, Cl₂, Br₂ and I₂; uses of ethyne.
- **Chapter 12E — Unit 9(vi), alcohols.** Ethanol preparation by hydrolysis of an alkyl halide; physical properties (nature, solubility, density, boiling points); chemical properties (combustion, sodium, ester formation with acetic acid, dehydration with concentrated sulphuric acid); denatured/methylated/spurious alcohol; uses.
- **Chapter 12F — Unit 9(vii), carboxylic acids.** Acetic acid structure; physical properties (vinegar odour, glacial acetic acid); chemical properties (litmus, alkalis, alcohols/esterification); uses.

**Verification status:** the scope lines were cross-checked against public 2026-27 syllabus summaries. The official CISCE PDF was not downloaded directly by the build environment. This limitation is repeated in `QA-REPORT.md`.

## 4. Standard chemistry references

The following standard facts were checked against multiple public chemistry references, the syllabus summaries above and the supplied pages:

- Carbon atomic number 6 and electronic configuration 2,4.
- Tetravalency: four covalent bonds. Catenation: self-linking into chains and rings.
- Single, double and triple bonds: one, two and three shared pairs.
- Methane CH₄, ethane C₂H₆, ethene C₂H₄, ethyne C₂H₂, benzene C₆H₆.
- Wöhler's 1828 synthesis of urea from ammonium cyanate.
- Typical organic versus inorganic property trends and their exceptions.
- Everyday examples: LPG, petrol, cellulose, proteins, aspirin, polythene, soap, urea.
- Environmental concerns: plastic persistence and combustion emissions.
- IUPAC naming conventions: longest chain, lowest locants, suffix priority and alphabetical substituent order.
- Alkane preparations from sodium ethanoate or sodium propanoate with soda lime; reduction of haloalkanes; aluminium carbide with water; the Wurtz reaction and its even-carbon limitation.
- Balanced combustion and chlorination equations, including limited-air combustion to carbon monoxide and soot.

No claim is made that these were copied from a specific prescribed textbook.

## 5. Deliberate omissions

The supplied book marks the following items as outside the syllabus, so they are omitted or mentioned only as non-examinable context:

- **Metamerism** — omitted from Chapter 12A.
- **Catalytic oxidation of alkanes** — omitted from Chapter 12B.
- **Slow combustion with acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ or KMnO₄** — omitted from Chapter 12B.
- **Alkenes and alkynes as taught topics** — out of scope for Chapter 12B; they belong to Chapters 12C and 12D.
- **Ozonolysis, polymerisation and KMnO₄ oxidation** — labelled enrichment or omitted from the required alkene content; the 2027 wording emphasises addition with H₂ and halogens.
- **HCl and ozone addition to ethyne** — labelled enrichment in Chapter 12D; the required properties are addition with H₂ and halogens.
- **Fermentation and industrial hydration** — labelled enrichment context in Chapter 12E; the required preparation is hydrolysis of an alkyl halide.
- **Oxidation, PCl₃ and ether formation** — labelled enrichment or omitted in Chapter 12E.
- **Preparation routes and extra acid reactions** — labelled enrichment or omitted in Chapter 12F; the required chemical properties are litmus, alkalis and alcohols.

Functional isomerism is included in Chapter 12A as a **labelled extension**, because the supplied book teaches it while the 2027 syllabus names chain and position isomerism.

Enrichment material, such as the tetrahedral bond angle of 109.5°, is labelled as enrichment.

## 9. Physics Chapter 1 — Force

**Official scope:** CISCE ICSE Physics 2027, Unit 1 Force, Work, Power and Energy, the Force section only.

- Turning forces concept; moment of a force, also called torque; SI and CGS units.
- Common examples: door, steering wheel, bicycle pedal, spanner.
- Clockwise and anticlockwise moments.
- Couple, couple arm and moment of couple.
- Equilibrium of bodies, static and dynamic; the two conditions for equilibrium.
- Principle of moments and its verification with a metre rule, support, spring balances and slotted weights.
- Centre of gravity: definition, qualitative location for regular bodies, and the plumb-line method for an irregular lamina.
- Uniform circular motion; speed versus velocity; centripetal and centrifugal force.

**Supplied source:** `content/physics/chapter-1-force/source/selina-class-10-physics-chapter-1-force.pdf` — 18 scanned pages of Selina ICSE Class 10 Physics Chapter 1 Force. The PDF has no text layer, so every page was read from a rendered page image. The scan states no edition or publication year, and no page mapping is claimed.

**Verification notes:**

- The syllabus box printed in the supplied chapter matches the 2027 Unit 1 Force scope for the topics used here.
- The official CISCE site returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval. The scope was cross-checked against the supplied chapter's syllabus box and current 2026-27 syllabus summaries.
- The supplied chapter also restates Class IX prerequisites (force as the rate of change of momentum, the newton and kgf). These are used only as prerequisite context, not as new teaching content.
- Centre of gravity is treated qualitatively, as required by the syllabus. The chapter does not derive centroid formulae beyond the standard positions listed.
- All 24 assessment questions and 15 quick checks are original practice questions. No textbook exercise or past-paper question was copied.
