## Official syllabus scope (CISCE ICSE 2027, Unit 9)

- **9(v) Hydrocarbons: alkanes, alkenes, alkynes.** Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons with a double bond; ethene as an example. Preparation of ethene by dehydrohalogenation and dehydration. Alkynes are unsaturated hydrocarbons with a triple bond; ethyne as an example. Preparation from calcium carbide and 1,2-dibromoethane (ethylene dibromide). Only main properties, particularly addition products with hydrogen and the halogens Cl₂, Br₂ and I₂, for alkenes and alkynes. Uses of methane, ethane, ethene and ethyne.
- **9(vi) Alcohols: ethanol — preparation, properties and uses.** Preparation by hydrolysis of an alkyl halide. Physical properties: nature, solubility, density, boiling points. Chemical properties: combustion, action with sodium, ester formation with acetic acid, dehydration with concentrated sulphuric acid to prepare ethene. Denatured, methylated alcohol or spirit and spurious alcohol. Important uses of ethanol.
- **9(vii) Carboxylic acids (aliphatic monocarboxylic acid): acetic acid — properties and uses.** Structure of acetic acid. Physical properties: odour (vinegar), glacial acetic acid. Chemical properties: action with litmus, alkalis and alcohol (idea of esterification). Uses of acetic acid.
- Supporting outcomes from 9(ii)–(iv): structural formulae up to five carbon atoms, chain and position isomerism, homologous-series patterns, and simple IUPAC nomenclature for the double bond, triple bond, alcoholic and carboxylic groups.

## Scope boundaries and enrichment

- **12C:** core examinable content is the alkene family, ethene structure, preparation by dehydration and dehydrohalogenation, addition with H₂/Cl₂/Br₂/I₂, and uses. Ozonolysis, polymerisation, oxidation with alkaline KMnO₄, and addition of water or HCl are labelled enrichment or omitted; they are not required by the 2027 wording.
- **12D:** core content is the alkyne family, ethyne structure, preparation from calcium carbide and 1,2-dibromoethane, addition with H₂/Cl₂/Br₂/I₂, combustion, uses, and the bromine/KMnO₄ distinction. Reactions with HCl and ozone are labelled enrichment. The cuprous chloride and silver nitrate tests are labelled extension tests.
- **12E:** core content is the alcohol family, ethanol preparation by hydrolysis of an alkyl halide, the required physical properties, combustion, sodium, esterification, dehydration, denatured/spurious alcohol and uses. Fermentation and industrial hydration are labelled enrichment context. Oxidation, PCl₃ and ether formation are labelled enrichment or omitted.
- **12F:** core content is the carboxylic acid family, acetic acid structure, vinegar and glacial acetic acid, litmus/alkali/alcohol reactions and uses. Preparation routes and reactions with metals, carbonates, bicarbonates, PCl₅, anhydrides and LiAlH₄ are labelled enrichment or omitted.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| alkene | alk- + -ene (C=C suffix) | Unsaturated aliphatic hydrocarbon with one C=C; CₙH₂ₙ | 12C L1 | -ene marks the double bond |
| olefin | oleum (oil) + facere (make) | Historical name for alkene | 12C L1 | Not the IUPAC name |
| unsaturated | un- + saturated | Contains a multiple bond; can add atoms | 12C L1 | Not about solution concentration |
| double bond | two shared pairs | C=C | 12C L1 | Not two separate single bonds |
| homologous series | homo (same) + logos (pattern) | Same general formula; successive members differ by CH₂ | 12C L1 | Similar, not identical |
| isomer | isos (equal) + meros (part) | Same molecular formula, different structure | 12C L1 | Not an isotope |
| chain isomerism | chain + isomerism | Different carbon skeleton | 12C L1 | Formula unchanged |
| position isomerism | position + isomerism | Same skeleton; multiple bond/group in a different position | 12C L1 | Skeleton unchanged |
| planar | planus (flat) | All atoms in one plane; ethene is planar | 12C L2 | A drawing alone does not prove planarity |
| dehydration | de- + hydro (water) | Removal of H and OH to form water | 12C L2 | Not simple drying |
| dehydrohalogenation | de- + hydro + halogen + -ation | Removal of H and halogen with hot alcoholic KOH | 12C L2 | Alcoholic KOH, not aqueous |
| hydrogenation | hydrogen + -ation | Addition of H₂ across a multiple bond | 12C L3 | Product is saturated |
| halogenation | halogen + -ation | Addition of Cl₂, Br₂ or I₂ | 12C L3 | Br₂ in CCl₄ is the standard test |
| catalyst | kata (down) + lysis (loosening) | Speeds a reaction without being used up overall | 12C L3 | May take part and regenerate |
| addition reaction | add + -ition | Atoms add across a multiple bond | 12C L3 | Not substitution |
| combustion | combustio (burning) | Reaction with oxygen releasing heat and light | 12C L4 | Sooty flame means incomplete combustion |
| cracking | crack + -ing | Breaking large hydrocarbons into smaller ones | 12C L1 | Not combustion |
| polymerisation | poly (many) + meros (part) | Many monomers joining into one large molecule | 12C L4 | Enrichment only for 2027 |
| ozonolysis | ozone + lysis (splitting) | Splitting a multiple bond with ozone | 12C L4 | Enrichment only for 2027 |
| aliphatic | aleiphar (fat) | Open-chain or non-aromatic organic compound | 12C L1 | Not “made of fat” |
| alkyne | alk- + -yne (C≡C suffix) | Unsaturated aliphatic hydrocarbon with one C≡C; CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ | 12D L1 | -yne marks the triple bond |
| triple bond | three shared pairs | C≡C | 12D L1 | Not three single bonds |
| acetylenic bond | acetylene + -ic | Historical name for C≡C | 12D L1 | Use “triple bond” or “alkyne” in IUPAC answers |
| carbide | carb- + -ide | Carbon combined with a metal, e.g. CaC₂ | 12D L2 | Not calcium carbonate |
| 1,2-dibromoethane | di- + bromo + ethane | C₂H₄Br₂ with one Br on each carbon | 12D L2 | Numbers show different carbons |
| purification | purus (clean) + facere (make) | Removing unwanted impurities | 12D L2 | Acidified CuSO₄/K₂Cr₂O₇ removes phosphine |
| ammoniacal | ammonia + -acal | Containing dissolved ammonia | 12D L4 | Not ammonia gas alone |
| cuprous | cuprum (copper) + -ous | Copper(I), Cu⁺ | 12D L4 | Cupric is Cu²⁺ |
| acetylide | acetylene + -ide | Compound from ethyne losing acidic H, e.g. CuC₂ | 12D L4 | Red precipitate is a terminal-alkyne test |
| tetrabromide | tetra (four) + bromide | Four bromine atoms added, C₂H₂Br₄ | 12D L3 | Addition can stop at the dibromide |
| oxy-acetylene | oxygen + acetylene | Very hot ethyne–oxygen flame for welding/cutting | 12D L4 | Heat comes from complete combustion in oxygen |
| Baeyer’s test | proper name (Adolf von Baeyer) | Decolourisation of cold dilute alkaline KMnO₄ | 12D L4 | Learn observation and meaning |
| alcohol | Arabic al-kuhl (historical) | Organic compound with –OH on carbon | 12E L1 | History does not predict chemistry |
| hydroxyl | hydro + oxy + -yl | The –OH group on carbon | 12E L1 | Not the hydroxide ion OH⁻ |
| monohydric | mono (one) + hydric (–OH) | One –OH group | 12E L1 | Not one hydrogen atom |
| dihydric | di (two) + hydric | Two –OH groups | 12E L1 | Groups may be on different carbons |
| trihydric | tri (three) + hydric | Three –OH groups | 12E L1 | One molecule, not three alcohols |
| fermentation | fervere (to boil) | Anaerobic enzymatic breakdown of sugar by yeast | 12E L2 | Bubbling, not boiling; enrichment |
| hydrolysis | hydro (water) + lysis (splitting) | Water splits a bond, e.g. RX + KOH(aq) → ROH | 12E L2 | Not hydration |
| alkyl halide | alkyl + halide | RX, halogen attached to an alkyl group | 12E L2 | Same family as haloalkane |
| haloalkane | halogen + alkane | Alkane with halogen replacing H | 12E L2 | Not a different compound family |
| esterification | ester + -ification | Acid + alcohol → ester + water with conc. H₂SO₄ | 12E L4 | Not ether formation |

| aldehyde | alcohol + dehydrogenatum | Compound with –CHO, e.g. ethanal | 12E L4 | Not an alcohol |

| volatile | volare (to fly) | Evaporates readily at room temperature | 12E L3 | Not “unstable” |

| antifreeze | anti- + freeze | Lowers the freezing point of water | 12E L5 | Does not prevent all cooling |
| carboxylic acid | carboxyl + acid | Organic acid containing –COOH | 12F L1 | Only the –COOH hydrogen is acidic |
| carboxyl group | carbonyl + hydroxyl | –COOH group | 12F L1 | Includes the –OH, not just C and O |
| monocarboxylic acid | mono + carboxyl + acid | One –COOH group | 12F L1 | Says nothing about chain length |
| dicarboxylic acid | di + carboxyl + acid | Two –COOH groups, e.g. oxalic acid | 12F L1 | Do not double-count carboxyl carbons |
| acetic acid | acetum (vinegar) + acid | Ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH | 12F L2 | Trivial name; ethanoic is IUPAC |
| vinegar | vinum (wine) + aigre (sour) | 4–5% aqueous acetic acid | 12F L2 | A mixture, not pure acid |
| glacial acetic acid | glacies (ice) + -al | Pure acetic acid freezing at about 17 °C | 12F L2 | Not from a glacier |
| hygroscopic | hygros (wet) + skopein (observe) | Absorbs moisture from air | 12F L2 | Not the same as deliquescent |
| alkaline | al-qali (ashes) + -ine | pH greater than 7; basic | 12F L3 | Opposite of acidic |
| litmus | lichen-derived dye | Indicator: red in acid, blue in alkali | 12F L3 | Not a formula |
| weak acid | weak + acid | Partially ionised in water | 12F L3 | Not the same as dilute |
| anhydride | an- (without) + hydr (water) | Acid with water removed | 12F L4 | Enrichment only |
| acetyl chloride | acetyl + chloride | CH₃COCl | 12F L4 | Enrichment; not ethyl chloride |
| reduction | re- + ducere (lead back) | Loss of oxygen, gain of hydrogen or gain of electrons | 12F L4 | Enrichment only |
| ferric chloride | ferrum (iron) + -ic | Iron(III) chloride, FeCl₃ | 12F L4 | Enrichment; ferrous is iron(II) |
